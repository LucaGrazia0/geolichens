about=Ranks candidate locations for historical lichen records using locality descriptions, ITALIC ecological profiles and user-supplied environmental layers. Provides candidate inspection, manual refinement, uncertainty estimates and provenance reports.
    Requires QGIS 3.40 through 4.2 with Python, NumPy and GDAL (normally included in QGIS desktop installations). No additional pip packages or QGIS plugins are required.
    Internet access is required for ITALIC and OpenStreetMap/Overpass queries. Locality search uses a configurable Photon service based on OpenStreetMap, with cached and rate-limited requests; no MapTiler key is required. The public Photon demo is for moderate interactive use, without availability guarantees. Configure your own Photon endpoint for intensive use.
    Intended for Italian locality descriptions and Italian lichens, with a default search area of Lombardy. Interface language: Italian. Datasets, optional local dictionaries, taxon indexes, environmental layers and DEMs are not bundled. Local dictionary enhancements are unavailable unless supplied separately.
    Results are model estimates requiring expert review. Automated tests passed on Linux/QGIS 3.40.15 and 4.2.3 and macOS/QGIS 3.44.9. Windows and full interactive QGIS 4 acceptance remain pending. No scientific accuracy or external service availability guarantee is implied.
